Privacy Policy - Crystal Siege: Endless Defense

Last updated: 30 October 2026

simplifyBytes ("we") publishes the mobile game Crystal Siege: Endless Defense (the "Game") on Google Play.

Data the Game itself handles

The Game does not require an account. Game progress (shards, permanent upgrades, best level, time and score, total kills, daily-streak date and settings) is stored only on your device and is erased by clearing the Game's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game. We do not operate our own servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows advertisements (a small banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a finished run, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to revive or to double a reward) through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's advertising identifier, coarse device and app information, ad interaction, crash and diagnostic data, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Analytics (Google Firebase)

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) to understand how it is played: which levels are reached, when ads are shown or watched, and estimated ad revenue per impression. This involves a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, your device's advertising identifier where permitted, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not use it to identify you. Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Google Play Games and ratings

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the Game uses Google Play Games Services (Google LLC) to show a high-score leaderboard. Google shares your Play Games gamer name, avatar and player ID with the Game, and the Game sends your best score to Google so it appears on the leaderboard. Signing in is optional and the Game works fully without it; you control who sees your Play Games profile in the Play Games app, and you can delete your Play Games data there or in your Google Account. The Game may also show Google Play's in-app rating card, which is handled entirely by Google Play. Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent and your choices

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the Game shows a consent form through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time from the Game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Declining consent does not limit the Game.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. We retain no personal data ourselves. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.
